WHY OPEN AI?

Memory isn't a dataset to hand over.

Echoes keeps each person's account separate. In private mode, Gemma runs through Ollama on your own computer and suggests a question from the stories you choose to save. It does not speak as the person you remember or decide whose version is true.

The optional judge demo sends one fixed fictional story to hosted Gemma through Vercel AI Gateway. It never sends your relay. Saved stories stay in this browser's local storage. The page uses system fonts and makes no analytics or third-party font requests.

A suggestion is only a suggestion. People keep authorship.
